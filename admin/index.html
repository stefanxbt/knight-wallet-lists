          Feature switches

          Show a feature to everyone, only to the real-money testers below, or hide it, without a new app build. Use it when something breaks or isn't released yet. Hidden means its buttons, rows and banners disappear; Sending and Swaps stay on screen and say they are paused. Phones pick a change up within about 5 minutes and remember it even without internet. New features start as testers only until you switch them on. This only shows or hides things: sending real money stays locked until the security audit, whatever this says. Admins only.
