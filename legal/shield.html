Safety checks notice

Knight Wallet · Draft version 0.9 · 1 November 2026

Knight checks tokens, websites, addresses and transactions using Dyrection Shield, GoPlus Security, public scam lists (MetaMask eth-phishing-detect and ScamSniffer) and its own rules.

  • They are warnings, not guarantees. A check can miss a new scam, or flag something that is safe.
  • No warning does not mean safe. Always check who you are dealing with.
  • You decide. Knight may stop you from signing something that looks clearly harmful, but in most cases you choose whether to go ahead.
  • Safety scores are opinions based on automated data, not a rating by a regulator or a promise about a token.
  • Dyrection is not liable for losses from scams that a check did not catch, to the extent the law allows.
