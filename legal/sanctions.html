Sanctions and restricted countries

Knight Wallet · Draft version 0.9 · 1 November 2026 · Awaiting lawyer review

Dyrection LLC follows the sanctions laws that apply to it, including those of the Cayman Islands (which apply United Kingdom sanctions), the United Nations, the United States (OFAC), the European Union and the United Arab Emirates. Knight is self-custody software, but we still do not offer it, or any feature that earns us a fee, to sanctioned places or people.

1. Countries and regions where Knight is not offered

Knight is not published in the app stores of these places, and its swap, trading, betting, browser and WalletConnect features are switched off when the app detects one of them:

Place Why
Cuba US comprehensive embargo
Iran UN, US, EU, UK sanctions; FATF call for action
North Korea (DPRK) UN, US, EU, UK sanctions; FATF call for action
Syria Remaining targeted US, EU and UK sanctions and export controls; kept blocked as a precaution while rules are eased
Russia Wide US, EU and UK sanctions, including EU bans on crypto-asset services to Russian residents
Belarus Wide US, EU and UK sanctions, including EU bans on crypto-asset services to Belarusian residents
Myanmar FATF call for action; targeted sanctions
Crimea, and the Russian-occupied parts of the Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson regions of Ukraine US, EU and UK comprehensive regional sanctions. These areas cannot be told apart by country code, so you must not use Knight there

2. Features limited in more countries

Some features are switched off in further countries because local rules restrict them (for example perpetual futures in the United States, United Kingdom and Canada, and prediction markets in the United States, United Kingdom and France). The app shows when a feature is not available where you are. The third-party providers apply their own country lists too.

3. People and addresses

You may not use Knight if you, or anyone who owns or controls you, are on a sanctions list. Knight may refuse to prepare swaps or trades involving blockchain addresses listed by OFAC or other authorities.

4. Your promise

By using Knight you confirm that you are not in a place listed above, are not a sanctioned person, and will not use Knight to evade sanctions, for example with a VPN.

5. Updates

Sanctions change often. We review this list at least every 3 months and whenever a major change happens. Last reviewed: 1 November 2026.
