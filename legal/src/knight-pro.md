# Knight Pro subscription terms

**Knight Wallet** · Draft version 0.9 · 1 November 2026

1. **What you get.** Knight Pro unlocks the extra features listed on the Knight Pro screen in the app before you subscribe. Your wallet, sending and receiving stay free without Pro.
2. **Price.** USD 4.99 per month, or the local price shown by the App Store or Google Play, including any taxes they apply.
3. **Billing.** You pay through your Apple ID or Google account. Dyrection never sees your card details.
4. **Auto-renewal.** Your subscription renews automatically each month unless you turn it off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged within 24 hours before renewal.
5. **How to cancel.** iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Knight Wallet > Cancel. Android: Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Knight Wallet > Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
6. **Free trials.** If a free trial is offered, it turns into a paid subscription at the end unless you cancel before it ends.
7. **Refunds.** Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their rules. If you are a consumer in the EU or UK, you have a 14-day right to withdraw; by starting Pro straight away you agree that the service begins immediately, which may limit that right as the store's rules allow.
8. **Price changes.** We will tell you in advance; Apple or Google will ask for your consent where required.
9. **Changes to Pro.** We may add, change or remove Pro features. If we remove a main feature, you can cancel and ask the store for a refund under its rules.
10. **No financial benefit.** Pro features are software tools. They do not give you any share, interest or return.
