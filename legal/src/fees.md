# Fees

**Knight Wallet** · Draft version 0.9 · 1 November 2026

Knight is free to download and free to hold, send and receive crypto. You always see every fee before you confirm.

| What you do | Knight's fee | Other costs you may pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hold, send, receive | None | Network fee (gas), paid to the blockchain |
| Swap or bridge | 0.5% of the swap amount today (never more than 1%), included in the quote and shown as "Knight fee". [CHECK: final public rate before launch] | Network fees; fees of the exchanges and bridges in the route; price impact |
| Perpetual futures and spot trades (Hyperliquid) | 0.1% of the order value (Hyperliquid "builder fee"), approved by you once and shown on every order | Hyperliquid's trading and funding fees |
| Prediction markets (Polymarket) | None today. If this changes, the fee is shown before you confirm | Polymarket's fees and spread; network fees |
| Buy or sell with card (Ramp, MoonPay) | None charged by Knight [CHECK: partner revenue share, if any, must be disclosed] | The partner's fees and spread |
| Knight Pro | USD 4.99 per month (local price shown in the store) | None |

**Where fees go.** Knight's fees are paid on-chain to Dyrection's fee wallets as part of the transaction you sign. Network fees go to the blockchain's validators, not to us.

**Changes.** We may change fees. New fees always appear in the quote before you confirm, and important changes are announced in the app.
