# Invite friends: programme terms

**Knight Wallet** · Draft version 0.9 · 1 November 2026 · NOT LIVE: the reward design must be confirmed by a lawyer and checked against Apple rule 3.1.5(v) before it is switched on.

1. **How it works.** You can share your personal invite link. When a friend installs Knight with your link and makes a qualifying swap, you can earn a reward.
2. **Reward.** [TO DECIDE: Apple does not allow crypto rewards for getting others to download an app. Proposed: free Knight Pro months, or a lower Knight fee on your own swaps.]
3. **Fair use.** No self-invites, fake accounts, spam, paid ads using our name, or misleading claims. We may cancel rewards that break these rules.
4. **No investment.** Rewards are a thank-you, not income, interest or a share in Dyrection.
5. **Changes.** We may change or end the programme at any time. Rewards already earned under the old rules will be honoured.
6. **Taxes.** You are responsible for any taxes on rewards.
