# Tester terms

**Knight Wallet test builds** · Draft version 0.9 · 1 November 2026

These terms apply to test versions of Knight (TestFlight, the Android test download and any other pre-release build), in addition to the [Terms of Use](terms.html).

1. **It is test software.** It may have bugs, lose settings, show wrong numbers or stop working. Always keep your recovery phrase written down safely.
2. **Real money can be lost.** If you are an approved real-money tester, you use real assets at your own risk. Knight limits real-money tests to USD 100 per action and USD 500 per day, and refuses unlimited token approvals, but limits do not remove the risk.
3. **No security audit yet.** Test builds have not had an independent security audit.
4. **Features may change or disappear** at any time, including features that are only shown to testers.
5. **Please keep it private.** Do not publish screenshots of unreleased features or share the test build without permission.
6. **Feedback.** If you send feedback, we may use it to improve Knight without paying you.
7. **No support promise.** We try to help, but we do not promise a response time.
8. **We can end testing** or remove your tester access at any time.
